Admissions

Our admissions process is designed to help families understand our school values and fit.

Step 1: Submit Inquiry

Start by sharing your student's details and goals through our inquiry form.

Step 2: Campus Visit

Attend a guided campus visit to meet faculty and explore facilities.

Step 3: Application Review

Submit previous records and complete a short family interview.

Requirements and Timeline

  • Completed inquiry/application form
  • Recent academic records
  • Immunization and supporting documents
  • Rolling review with responses in 2-3 weeks
